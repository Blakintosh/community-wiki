    <h2 id="about-heading">A single home for CoD modding knowledge</h2>
    <p>Technical and general information on modding Call of Duty, with a primary focus on <strong>Black Ops III</strong> — the only title in the franchise to ship official mod tools — while welcoming contributions for any other CoD title, including those without an official SDK.</p>

    <h2 id="contrib-heading">Four steps to a merged page</h2>
    <ol class="steps">
      <li><span class="steps__n">1</span><span><strong>Fork the repo</strong> on GitHub. New to forks? <a href="https://docs.github.com/en/get-started/quickstart/fork-a-repo" target="_blank" rel="noopener">GitHub's guide</a> walks you through it.</span></li>
      <li><span class="steps__n">2</span><span><strong>Add your page</strong> under <code>_docs/</code>. Copy an existing one for structure.</span></li>
      <li><span class="steps__n">3</span><span><strong>Register it</strong> in <code>_data/toc.yml</code> so it appears in the sidebar.</span></li>
      <li><span class="steps__n">4</span><span><strong>Open a pull request.</strong> We'll review, polish, and merge.</span></li>
    </ol>
    <p style="margin-top:16px">If that workflow is daunting, drop your draft with images and assets in <a href="https://discord.gg/tzzn6zAVBz" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Discord</a> or <a href="{{ site.repo }}/issues/new" target="_blank" rel="noopener">open an issue</a> and we'll handle the rest.</p>
